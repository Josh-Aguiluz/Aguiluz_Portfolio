# Coffee & Cream Color Palette Reference

## 🎨 Official Warm Minimalist Colors

### Primary Colors
- **Cream Background**: `#FAF8F5` (light) / `#1A1715` (dark)
- **Dark Espresso Text**: `#3E3632` (light) / `#E2E8F0` (dark)
- **Muted Sand Accent**: `#D6CFC4` (light) / `#B8B0A6` (dark)
- **Gold Highlight**: `#D4AF37` (sparingly used)

### Supporting Warm Tones
- **Light Warm**: `#F0EBE6` (backgrounds)
- **Mid Warm**: `#E8E4DF` (subtle backgrounds)
- **Warm Brown**: `#6B5D52` (contact section)
- **Medium Brown**: `#8A7868` (dot matrix)

## 📝 Usage Guidelines

### Text
- **Primary Text**: `#3E3632` (almost black warm grey)
- **Secondary Text**: `#6B5D52` (medium warm brown)
- **Muted Text**: `#8A7868` (light warm brown)

### Backgrounds
- **Page BG**: `#FAF8F5` (cream)
- **Card BG**: `#FFFFFF` (white) or `#F0EBE6` (warm tint)
- **Section BG**: `#E8E4DF` (subtle warm)

### Accents & Buttons
- **Primary Button**: `#D6CFC4` (muted sand)
- **Secondary Button**: `#E8E4DF` (light warm)
- **Hover State**: Add 20% opacity to muted sand
- **Borders**: `#D6CFC4` (subtle, not bright)

### Decorative Elements
- **Marquee Borders**: `#D6CFC4` (muted sand)
- **Card Borders**: `#D6CFC4` or `#E8E4DF`
- **Code Tags**: `#D6CFC4` background with `#3E3632` text
- **Icons**: Use warm grays and browns only

## 🌙 Dark Mode Adjustments
- Background: `#1A1715` (very dark warm brown)
- Text: `#E2E8F0` (light warm grey)
- Accents: `#B8B0A6` (darker muted sand)
- Borders: `#3E3632` (espresso)

## ✅ The "Coffee & Cream" Principle
Every color should feel warm, professional, and academic—like coffee, cream, paper, and wood. No blues, no greens, only warm earth tones.
